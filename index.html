Ages of Rule

A timeline of the ways people have governed themselves. The chart counts the systems alive in each year. Drag the marker to see which ones.

Each system counts once, whatever its size, and some entries are types of rule rather than states. The record leans toward Europe, the Mediterranean and Asia. What is missing.

Countries start at their independence or founding year and only states in CountryData are counted, so the steep rise after 1900 is partly an artefact of that.
